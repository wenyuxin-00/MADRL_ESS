Hi everyone, good afternoon. I'm Yuxin Wen and today is my master thesis presentation. the title of the thesis is: Building Energy management system based on Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement learning. 
The presentation will be devided into these 4 parts. firstly is the introduction and scope of the research.
本研究关注低压配电网中多个 prosumer 的协同能源管理问题。随着住宅侧 PV、stationary battery 和 EV charging 的增加，用户侧资源具有更高的灵活性，但也引入了新的耦合问题。一方面，电池和 EV 可以根据电价进行调度，从而降低运行成本；另一方面，多个用户同时充电或充放电，会影响整体的电压、线路负载以及变压器负载。

This research focuses on coordinated energy management for multiple prosumers in a low-voltage distribution grid. With more residential PV, stationary batteries, and EV charging, demand-side resources become much more flexible, but they also create new coupling issues. On the one hand, batteries and EVs can be scheduled according to electricity prices to reduce operating cost. On the other hand, if several users charge and discharge at the same time, they can affect voltage, line loading, and transformer loading in the distribution grid.

因此，本研究的核心问题是：如何让多个 prosumer 在分布式执行的条件下，同时考虑经济性、EV 的出行需求以及配电网安全？为此，我构建了一个基于 pandapower 的低压配电网环境，并主要使用 MADRL 完成控制器训练和实验比较。

So the core question of this thesis is: under decentralized execution, how can multiple prosumers coordinate their decisions while considering cost, EV mobility requirements, and grid safety at the same time? To answer this question, I built a low-voltage grid environment based on pandapower, and mainly used MADRL to train and compare different controllers.

then, lets come to the system modelling.
首先定义系统问题。本研究假设有 3 个 prosumer，每个 prosumer 都包含 household load、PV、stationary battery 和 EV。每个 prosumer 的净负荷由本地 household 负荷、PV 利用功率、电池功率以及 EV 充电功率共同决定。其中，电池功率为正表示充电，为负表示放电；EV 在本研究中只考虑 charging，不考虑 V2G。

First, I define the system problem. In this study, there are three prosumers. Each prosumer has household load, PV, a stationary battery, and one electrical vehicle. The net load of each prosumer is determined by the local household load, the utilized PV power, the battery power, and the EV charging power. Here, positive battery power means charging, and negative battery power means discharging. For the EV, this study only considers charging and does not include Vehicle to grid.

由于考虑经济性，优化目标是最小化所有 prosumer 在整个 episode 中的总运行成本。这个目标不仅包含 EV charging cost，也包含电池 charging cost 和 discharging benefit。同时，系统还需要满足三类约束：第一是电池和 EV 的 SoC 约束，第二是 EV 离家时的 SoC requirement，第三是配电网安全约束，包括电压、线路负载和变压器负载。

Since economic performance is important, the objective is to minimize the total operating cost of all prosumers over the whole episode. This objective includes not only the EV charging cost, but also the battery charging cost and the discharging benefit. At the same time, the system must satisfy three types of constraints: first, the SoC constraints of batteries and EVs; second, the EV departure SoC requirement; and third, grid safety constraints.

这个问题的难点在于，它不是一个单步决策问题。电池 SoC 和 EV SoC 会把当前动作和未来可行域耦合起来；多个 prosumer 的动作又会通过 power flow 在电网中相互耦合。因此，控制器既要考虑本地设备状态，也要考虑全局电网约束。

So, the difficulty is that this is not a single-step decision problem. Battery SoC and EV SoC couple the current action with the future feasible region. At the same time, the actions of different prosumers are coupled through power flow in the grid. Therefore, the controller has to consider both local device states and global grid constraints.

系统工作流程可以概括为一个闭环。首先，环境读取负荷、PV 和电价数据，并由 LSTM 生成未来 horizon 的预测序列。然后，每个 MADRL actor 基于本地 observation 输出三维动作，分别对应 battery、PV 和 EV。

The system workflow can be summarized as a closed loop. First, the environment reads load, PV, and price data, and the LSTM generates forecast sequences for the future horizon. Then, each MADRL actor takes its local observation and outputs a three-dimensional action, corresponding to the battery, PV, and EV.

这些动作不会直接执行，而是先经过本地可行性检查；如果启用 safety projection，还会进一步修正 battery power 和 PV curtailment。随后环境更新 SoC 和净负荷，并调用 pandapower 进行 AC power flow。最后，环境根据经济收益、EV 约束、电池正则化和电网安全状态计算 reward，并把 transition 存入 replay buffer 用于训练。

These actions are not executed directly. They first go through local feasibility checks. If safety projection is enabled, battery power and PV curtailment can be further adjusted. After that, the environment updates SoC and net load, and calls pandapower to run AC power flow. Finally, the environment calculates the reward based on economic return, EV constraints, battery regularization, and grid safety status, and stores the transition in the replay buffer for training.

EV 是本研究里最重要的柔性负荷。每个 prosumer 配置一辆 EV，EV 在晚上到家、第二天早上离家，因此连接窗口跨越午夜。EV 到家时 SoC 被重置为较低水平，离家时要求达到较高的目标 SoC，这代表用户的 mobility requirement。由于本研究不考虑 V2G，EV 在连接期间只能吸收电能，不能向建筑或电网放电。

The EV is the most important flexible load in this study. Each prosumer has one EV. The EV arrives in the evening and leaves the next morning, so the connection window crosses midnight. When the EV arrives, its SoC is reset to a relatively low level, and when it leaves, it must reach a higher target SoC. This represents the user's mobility requirement. Since V2G is not considered here, the EV can only absorb energy during the connection period and cannot discharge to the building or the grid.

MADRL actor 输出的 EV action 会被映射为一个充电功率请求。只有当 EV connected 时，这个请求才有效；否则 EV 充电功率为零。之后环境会根据 charger power limit 和 EV SoC headroom 进行裁剪，防止 EV 超过最大 SoC。

The EV action produced by the MADRL actor is mapped to a charging power request. This request is only valid when the EV is connected; otherwise, the EV charging power is set to zero. Then the environment clips the request according to the charger power limit and the EV SoC headroom, so that the EV does not exceed its maximum SoC.

EV SoC 的更新是线性的，由实际充电功率、时间步长、充电效率和 EV 容量共同决定。从能量需求上看，EV 从到家 SoC 充到离家目标 SoC 需要相当明显的电量补充，因此 EV 约束不是一个很轻的约束，它会显著影响夜间负荷和电池调度。

The EV SoC update is linear. It depends on the actual charging power, the time step length, the charging efficiency, and the EV battery capacity. From the energy-demand perspective, charging the EV from the arrival SoC to the departure target SoC requires a substantial amount of energy. So the EV constraint is not a light constraint. It has a clear impact on nighttime load and battery scheduling.

每个 prosumer 也配有 stationary battery。电池是系统中主要的经济调度资源：低价时充电，高价时放电，从而产生 arbitrage profit。PV 模型中，actor 不改变可用 PV 本身，而是决定 PV utilization factor。未被利用的部分被视为 curtailment。当前模型没有显式 PV curtailment cost，因此 PV curtailment 主要通过改变 net load 和 power flow 间接影响 reward。

Each prosumer also has a stationary battery. The battery is the main economic scheduling resource in the system: it charges when the price is low and discharges when the price is high, creating arbitrage profit. For PV, the actor does not change the available PV power itself. Instead, it decides the PV utilization factor. The unused part is treated as curtailment. In the current model, there is no explicit PV curtailment cost, so PV curtailment mainly affects the reward indirectly by changing net load and power flow.

为了让 controller 能够看到未来信息，项目使用 LSTM 预测电价、负荷和 PV。以电价预测为例，LSTM 可以捕捉整体价格趋势，但对局部峰值和谷值存在 smoothing effect。这一点对控制很重要：如果高价被低估，agent 可能低估放电收益；如果低价被高估，agent 可能错过充电机会。因此，后续 MADRL 的经济性能不仅取决于强化学习算法本身，也会受到预测误差影响。

To give the controller future information, the project uses LSTM models to forecast electricity price, load, and PV. Taking price prediction as an example, the LSTM can capture the overall price trend, but it has a smoothing effect on local peaks and valleys. This matters for control: if a high price is underestimated, the agent may underestimate the value of discharging; if a low price is overestimated, the agent may miss a good charging opportunity. Therefore, the economic performance of MADRL depends not only on the reinforcement learning algorithm itself, but also on the forecast errors.

MADRL observation 分为三个部分。第一部分是 local observation，描述当前物理状态，包括电池 SoC、EV SoC、EV 是否连接、当前负荷、PV 和电价。第二部分是 forecast sequence，用于让 actor 感知未来电价、负荷和 PV 的变化。例如，电价序列可以帮助 actor 判断当前时间步更适合充电还是放电。第三部分是 safety observation，主要服务于 feasibility check 和 safety projection，包含设备容量、功率上限和当前 SoC，因此可以判断 action 是否会导致物理不可行。

The MADRL observation has three parts. The first part is the local observation, which describes the current physical state, including battery SoC, EV SoC, EV connection status, current load, PV, and price. The second part is the forecast sequence, which allows the actor to see future changes in price, load, and PV. For example, the price sequence helps the actor decide whether the current time step is more suitable for charging or discharging. The third part is the safety observation, mainly used for feasibility checks and safety projection. It includes device capacity, power limits, and current SoC, so it can indicate whether an action may become physically infeasible.

每个 agent 的 action 是三维连续动作。Battery action 控制充放电，PV action 控制利用率，EV action 控制充电功率。一个关键设计是，battery action 不直接乘以最大功率，而是先根据当前 SoC 计算本时间步可行的 power interval。这样即使 actor 输出边界动作，执行动作也不会直接导致 battery SoC 越界。这种 action mapping 降低了学习难度，因为 critic 不需要学习大量由物理不可行动作导致的无效反馈。

Each agent has a three-dimensional continuous action. The battery action controls charging and discharging, the PV action controls utilization, and the EV action controls charging power. One key design is that the battery action is not simply multiplied by the maximum power. Instead, the feasible power interval is first calculated based on the current SoC. In this way, even if the actor outputs a boundary action, the executed action will not directly violate the battery SoC limit. This action mapping reduces the learning difficulty, because the critic does not need to learn many invalid feedback signals caused by physically infeasible actions.

Reward 设计同时包含经济目标、EV mobility requirement、电池行为和电网安全。Battery reward 鼓励低价充电、高价放电；EV cost 使 agent 避免不必要的高价充电；departure penalty 用于约束离家 SoC；progress penalty 提供更密集的 EV 充电引导；price-aware penalty 用于减少高价时段充电。

The reward design includes economic objectives, EV mobility requirements, battery behavior, and grid safety. The battery reward encourages charging at low prices and discharging at high prices. The EV cost discourages unnecessary high-price charging. The departure penalty enforces the EV departure SoC. The progress penalty provides denser guidance for EV charging. The price-aware penalty is used to reduce EV charging during high-price periods.

这里需要强调，reward 不是单纯为了最小成本。它实际上在塑造 agent 的学习方向。如果某个约束只在 episode 的少数时间步给出反馈，比如 departure penalty 只在离家时刻触发，那么学习信号会比较 sparse，actor 很难稳定学到整晚的充电节奏。这也是后续 soft 线中引入 progress penalty 的原因。

Here I want to emphasize that the reward is not only about minimizing cost. It also shapes the learning direction of the agent. If a constraint only gives feedback at a few time steps in an episode, for example if the departure penalty is only triggered when the EV leaves, then the learning signal is very sparse. In that case, it is difficult for the actor to learn a stable charging rhythm over the whole night. This is why I later introduce the progress penalty in the soft-control line.

Safety projection 是 reward penalty 之外的硬修正机制。它不是等 unsafe action 执行后再惩罚，而是在动作执行前直接修正动作。投影器通过有限差分估计 voltage 和 line loading 对 net injection 的 sensitivity，然后把安全约束写成 half-space，并逐行进行 projection。

Safety projection is a hard correction mechanism beyond reward penalties. It does not wait until an unsafe action is executed and then penalize it. Instead, it directly modifies the action before execution. The projector estimates the sensitivity of voltage and line loading to net injection using finite differences, then writes the safety constraints as half-spaces and projects the action step by step.

这会带来一个重要影响：actor 输出的 raw action 和环境中实际执行的 action 不再完全一致。因此 projection 虽然提升电网安全，但也可能改变 policy gradient 看到的 action-reward relationship，使 actor 学到的 battery arbitrage 策略被频繁裁剪。这一点是后面分析 projection 经济性下降的关键。

This creates an important effect: the raw action produced by the actor and the action actually executed in the environment are no longer exactly the same. So although projection improves grid safety, it can also change the action-reward relationship seen by the policy gradient. As a result, the battery arbitrage strategy learned by the actor may be frequently clipped or modified. This is the key reason behind the later decrease in economic performance under projection.

MADRL 采用 centralized training and decentralized execution。执行时，每个 actor 只使用自己的 observation；训练时，critic 接收所有 agents 的 joint observation 和 joint action，从而学习 agents 之间通过电网产生的耦合影响。

MADRL follows centralized training and decentralized execution. During execution, each actor only uses its own observation. During training, the critic receives the joint observations and joint actions of all agents, so it can learn the coupling effects among agents through the power grid.

训练算法接近 multi-agent TD3。每个 agent 有 actor 和 twin critics，以减少 Q-value overestimation。Replay buffer 使用 n-step return，其中 n-step 的时间长度可以覆盖 EV 夜间充电窗口中的长时序影响。Actor update 时，只替换 joint action 中当前 agent 的动作，其他 agent 的动作保持 batch 中的值。这使 critic 可以评估单个 agent 政策改进对联合系统的边际影响。

The training algorithm is close to multi-agent TD3. Each agent has an actor and twin critics, which helps reduce Q-value overestimation. The replay buffer uses n-step returns, and the n-step horizon is long enough to cover long-term effects in the overnight EV charging window. During actor update, only the current agent's action in the joint action is replaced, while the other agents' actions remain from the batch. This allows the critic to evaluate the marginal effect of one agent's policy improvement on the joint system.

Baseline 用于说明 MADRL 相对简单规则和优化方法的优劣。Local MPC 每个 prosumer 独立求解，因此经济性通常较好，但缺少 network-level coordination。ADMM-MPC 在 Local MPC 基础上增加 transformer aggregation constraint，因此安全性更好。

The baselines are used to show the strengths and weaknesses of MADRL compared with rule-based and optimization-based methods. Local MPC solves the problem independently for each prosumer, so it usually has good economic performance, but it lacks network-level coordination. ADMM-MPC adds a transformer aggregation constraint on top of Local MPC, so it performs better in terms of safety.

Rule-based controller 则作为简单参考。会根据电价分位数进行电池充放电。这类方法计算成本低，但不能主动利用 forecast，也不能学习 load、PV、price、EV SoC 和 grid 状态之间的复杂关系。

The rule-based controller is used as a simple reference. It charges and discharges the battery according to price quantiles. This type of method has low computational cost, but it cannot actively use forecasts, and it cannot learn the complex relationship among load, PV, price, EV SoC, and grid state.

实验部分首先验证 MADRL 的训练稳定性。对比短训练和长训练可以看到，训练轮数不足时，reward 波动较明显，critic loss 也不够稳定；在更长训练后，策略收益明显改善，critic 的估计也更加平稳。因此后续实验采用1000episode的训练设置作为主要比较对象。

In the experiment section, I first check the training stability of MADRL. By comparing short and long training, we can see that when the number of training episodes is not enough, the reward still fluctuates a lot, and the critic loss is not very stable. After longer training, the policy return improves clearly, and the critic estimation also becomes more stable. Therefore, the later experiments mainly use the 1000-episode training setting for comparison.

接下来进入实验部分，首先看 soft EV constraint。Soft mode 的特点是，EV 充电功率主要由 actor 输出，EV 离家 SoC 要求通过 reward penalty 来学习。因此它给了 policy 较大的自由度，理论上有利于学习低价充电和电池套利；但问题是 EV departure penalty 只在离家时刻触发，属于非常稀疏的学习信号，学习不稳定。

Next, I move to the main experimental results, starting with the soft EV constraint. In soft mode, the EV charging power mainly comes from the actor output, and the EV departure SoC requirement is learned through a reward penalty. This gives the policy more freedom, which in theory helps it learn low-price charging and battery arbitrage. 

在 soft base 中，MADRL 能学到很强的 battery arbitrage，因此经济性表现最好。但这个结果并不代表控制器足够稳健，因为它仍然会出现 EV 离家 SoC 不满足要求，EV departure SoC有两次低于目标。这是因为battery reward 是每个时间步都有的 dense signal，critic 很容易学习“什么时候充电、什么时候放电”带来的即时收益；而 EV departure constraint 只在少数时间点反馈，是sparse signal，Actor-critic训练中，critic更容易学习频繁且即时的battery arbitrage reward, 而EV的credit assignment 更困难。所以 actor 更倾向于优先优化电池收益，而不是稳定规划整晚的 EV 充电轨迹。

In the soft base case, MADRL learns strong battery arbitrage, so it has the best economic performance. But this does not mean the controller is robust enough, because the EV departure SoC can still fail to meet the target, and in this case it falls below the target twice. The reason is that the battery reward is a dense signal at every time step, so the critic can easily learn the immediate benefit of when to charge and when to discharge. In contrast, the EV departure constraint only gives feedback at a few time points, so it is a sparse signal. In actor-critic training, the critic learns the frequent and immediate battery arbitrage reward more easily, while credit assignment for the EV requirement is much harder. As a result, the actor tends to optimize battery profit first, instead of planning a stable EV charging trajectory over the whole night.

第一个改进是提高 EV cost weight，将EV charging cost 在reward中放大到1.5倍。这个方法确实改善了 EV feasibility，但经济性明显变差，电池套利收益也被削弱。原因不是简单的“EV 充电变贵了”，而是 reward scale 被改变后，critic 对 EV 相关动作的价值估计更加保守。为了避免未来的 departure penalty，agent 更倾向于提前、稳定地给 EV 充电，而不是等待更合适的低价时段。同时，EV charging 和 battery charging 都集中在夜间低价窗口，两者会竞争相同的灵活性资源，因此 EV 权重提高后，牺牲了battery arbitrage learning。

The first improvement is to increase the EV cost weight, so the EV charging cost is amplified by 1.5 times in the reward. This method does improve EV feasibility, but the economic performance becomes clearly worse, and the battery arbitrage profit is also weakened. The reason is not simply that EV charging becomes more expensive. More importantly, after the reward scale changes, the critic becomes more conservative when estimating the value of EV-related actions. To avoid future departure penalties, the agent tends to charge the EV earlier and more steadily, instead of waiting for better low-price periods. At the same time, EV charging and battery charging both compete for the same low-price nighttime window. So after the EV weight is increased, battery arbitrage learning is sacrificed.

相比之下，progress penalty 是更有效的 soft 改进。它不是单纯调大某个 reward 权重，而是在 EV 连接窗口内构建一条从到家 SoC 到目标 SoC 的参考轨迹。如果实际 SoC 落后于这条轨迹，就持续产生 penalty。这样做的算法意义是，把原来只在 departure step 出现的 sparse constraint 转换成整个夜间窗口内的 dense temporal guidance。Critic 能更早、更连续地看到 EV SoC 是否落后，actor 也更容易学到逐步充电策略，而不是等到最后再补救。因此，progress penalty 在满足 EV 出行需求的同时，比单纯提高 cost weight 保留了更好的经济性。

In comparison, the progress penalty is a more effective soft-control improvement. It does not simply increase one reward weight. Instead, it builds a reference trajectory from the arrival SoC to the target SoC during the EV connection window. If the actual SoC falls behind this trajectory, a penalty is continuously applied. From the algorithmic perspective, this converts the sparse constraint that only appears at the departure step into dense temporal guidance over the whole night. The critic can observe earlier and more continuously whether the EV SoC is falling behind, and the actor can more easily learn gradual charging instead of last-minute correction. Therefore, while satisfying the EV mobility requirement, the progress penalty keeps better economic performance than simply increasing the cost weight.

然后加入 grid safety projection。Projection + Progress 显著改善了电网安全，但经济性明显下降。这里的关键原因是 projection 改变了 actor 输出动作和环境实际执行动作之间的关系。Actor 可能希望根据电价进行较大功率的电池充放电，但当 power flow 接近安全边界时，projection 会修正 battery power 或 PV curtailment。于是 critic 看到的 reward 实际上由“原始动作意图”和“投影后的执行动作”共同决定，这会削弱 action-reward relationship 的一致性。对于 battery arbitrage 这种高度依赖时序和功率幅度的策略，这种 mismatch 会明显降低学习效果。

Then I add grid safety projection. Projection plus Progress significantly improves grid safety, but the economic performance drops clearly. The key reason is that projection changes the relationship between the action output by the actor and the action actually executed by the environment. The actor may want to charge or discharge the battery with high power according to the price signal. But when the power flow is close to the safety boundary, projection modifies battery power or PV curtailment. Therefore, the reward seen by the critic is actually determined by both the raw action intention and the projected executed action. This weakens the consistency of the action-reward relationship. For battery arbitrage, which strongly depends on timing and power magnitude, this mismatch can significantly reduce the learning effectiveness.

接下来进入 hard EV constraint。Hard mode 的核心思想是把 EV departure requirement 从 reward learning 转移到 action execution 层。每个时间步都会计算：如果之后都用最大充电功率，是否还能在离家前达到目标；如果不能，环境就强制执行一个最低充电功率。因此 actor 仍然可以选择更高的充电功率，但不能选择低于可行性要求的功率。最终执行功率是 actor 请求功率和 required minimum power 的最大值。也就是说，actor 仍然可以选择更高的充电功率，但不能选择低于维持可行性的功率。hard mode 减少了 EV feasibility 对 reward learning 的依赖，因此降低了 sparse reward 带来的 credit assignment 难度。但它也引入了 action correction，可能与 actor 的原始经济调度意图发生冲突。hard 线的实验逻辑和 soft 线不同。由于 EV feasibility 已经在执行层被处理，问题不再是“能否学会 departure requirement”，而是：在满足 EV hard constraint 的同时，是否还能保持 battery arbitrage、低价 EV charging 和 grid safety。

Next, I move to the hard EV constraint. The core idea of hard mode is to move the EV departure requirement from reward learning to the action execution layer. At each time step, the environment checks whether the EV can still reach the target before departure if it charges at maximum power in the remaining time. If not, the environment enforces a minimum required charging power. So the actor can still choose a higher charging power, but it cannot choose a power below the feasibility requirement. The final executed power is the maximum between the actor's requested power and the required minimum power. In other words, the actor still has freedom to charge more, but not less than what is needed to maintain feasibility. Hard mode reduces the dependence of EV feasibility on reward learning, so it reduces the credit assignment difficulty caused by sparse rewards. But it also introduces action correction, which may conflict with the actor's original economic scheduling intention. The logic of the hard-control experiments is different from the soft-control line. Since EV feasibility is already handled at the execution layer, the question is no longer whether the agent can learn the departure requirement. The question becomes: while satisfying the hard EV constraint, can the controller still maintain battery arbitrage, low-price EV charging, and grid safety?

Base Hard 的经济性较好，说明 hard correction 可以让 EV 充电更接近最低需求，而不是为了避免 penalty 过度保守充电。但它的问题是 grid safety 仍然不足。原因是 hard EV constraint 只处理单个 EV 的 mobility requirement，并不限制多个 prosumer 同时进行电池充放电。hard EV correction 解决的是局部 EV 可行性，但 critic 学到的经济最优策略仍然可能利用所有剩余 action freedom 进行 aggressive battery arbitrage。因此需要额外的 grid-level correction。

Base Hard has relatively good economic performance. This shows that hard correction can make EV charging closer to the minimum requirement, instead of overcharging conservatively just to avoid penalties. However, its problem is that grid safety is still not sufficient. The reason is that the hard EV constraint only handles the mobility requirement of each individual EV. It does not restrict several prosumers from charging or discharging their batteries at the same time. So hard EV correction solves local EV feasibility, but the economically optimal strategy learned by the critic may still use all remaining action freedom for aggressive battery arbitrage. Therefore, an additional grid-level correction is needed.

在 hard 线中加入 projection 和 cost weight 后，安全性改善，但经济性进一步下降。这是因为多个 correction 同时作用于策略：EV 动作会被 hard feasibility correction 修改，battery 动作又会被 safety projection 修改，cost weight 还改变了 reward landscape。最后，从 critic 估计的 Q 曲线可以看到，加入这些机制后，Q landscape 更容易出现 flat region 或不平滑变化。对于 battery arbitrage 这种依赖精确充放电时机的策略，这会使 actor gradient 更难稳定指向低价充电、高价放电的方向，所以 battery arbitrage learning 被削弱。

After adding projection and cost weight in the hard-control line, safety improves, but economic performance decreases further. This is because multiple corrections act on the policy at the same time: the EV action is modified by hard feasibility correction, the battery action is modified by safety projection, and the cost weight also changes the reward landscape. From the critic-estimated Q curve, we can see that after these mechanisms are added, the Q landscape is more likely to contain flat regions or non-smooth changes. For battery arbitrage, which depends on precise charging and discharging timing, this makes it harder for the actor gradient to consistently point toward low-price charging and high-price discharging. As a result, battery arbitrage learning is weakened.

Price-aware penalty的设计是：当当前电价高于episode内70%分位数，对EV charging增加额外的penalty，目的是减少高价时段 EV 充电。它可以在一定程度上改变充电时间分布，但效果有限。原因是 EV charging 的可调度空间已经被连接窗口和 hard feasibility 约束压缩；同时，grid projection 仍然限制 battery 对系统负荷的调节能力。因此 price-aware penalty 的边际作用被约束层削弱，并不能单独恢复整体经济性。

The price-aware penalty is designed as follows: when the current price is higher than the 70th percentile within the episode, an additional penalty is added to EV charging, with the goal of reducing EV charging during high-price periods. It can change the charging time distribution to some extent, but the effect is limited. The reason is that the schedulable space of EV charging has already been compressed by the connection window and the hard feasibility constraint. At the same time, grid projection still limits the battery's ability to compensate for system load. Therefore, the marginal effect of the price-aware penalty is weakened by the constraint layer, and it cannot restore the overall economic performance by itself.

Projection Hard 是 hard 线中最稳健的方案。它不额外增加 EV cost weight 或 price-aware penalty，而是把 EV 可行性和电网安全都放到 action execution 层处理。EV hard correction 负责保证离家 SoC，safety projection 负责避免电网越界，reward 本身则主要保留经济调度信号。这样 actor-critic 训练时面对的 reward 结构更干净，不需要在多个 penalty 之间反复权衡，所以还能保留一定的 battery arbitrage 能力。它的代价是，执行层修正偏保守，尤其是 EV hard correction 容易让 EV 充到高于最低需求的水平，因此 EV charging cost 会偏高。

Projection Hard is the most robust controller in the hard-control line. It does not add extra EV cost weight or price-aware penalty. Instead, it handles both EV feasibility and grid safety at the action execution layer. The EV hard correction is responsible for guaranteeing the departure SoC, while safety projection is responsible for avoiding grid violations. The reward itself mainly keeps the economic scheduling signal. In this way, during actor-critic training, the reward structure is cleaner, and the agent does not need to repeatedly trade off among multiple penalties. This is why it can still preserve some battery arbitrage capability. The cost is that execution-layer correction tends to be conservative, especially because EV hard correction can charge the EV above the minimum required level. Therefore, EV charging cost becomes higher.

最终选取四个代表性的 MADRL controller 进行比较。Soft Base 的 battery arbitrage 最强，storage profit 最高，但 EV feasibility 和 grid safety 不足。Soft Base + Progress 通过 dense EV guidance 解决了 EV departure 问题，并改善了 grid stress，是 soft framework 下较好的折中。

Finally, I compare four representative MADRL controllers. Soft Base has the strongest battery arbitrage and the highest storage profit, but its EV feasibility and grid safety are not sufficient. Soft Base plus Progress solves the EV departure problem through dense EV guidance and also reduces grid stress, so it is a better trade-off within the soft-control framework.

Base Hard 的 EV cost 最低，说明 hard feasibility correction 可以让 EV charging 更接近最低需求，同时保留较强 battery arbitrage。但它仍然存在 transformer overloading 风险。Projection Hard 则是最 robust 的方案：它实现了 0 voltage violation 和最低的 grid risk，同时仍然保持一定 battery profit，但代价是 EV charging cost 增加。

Base Hard has the lowest EV cost, which shows that hard feasibility correction can make EV charging closer to the minimum requirement while still keeping strong battery arbitrage. However, it still has transformer overloading risk. Projection Hard is the most robust solution: it achieves zero voltage violations and the lowest grid risk, while still keeping some battery profit. The trade-off is that EV charging cost increases.

因此，MADRL 的优势不是在所有指标上压倒 MPC，而是相比 rule-based controller 能学习更灵活的 price-aware 和 resource-coupling 策略；同时，通过 progress penalty 或 safety projection，可以把 EV feasibility 和 grid safety 纳入控制框架。

Therefore, the advantage of MADRL is not that it beats MPC on every metric. Its advantage is that, compared with rule-based controllers, it can learn more flexible price-aware and resource-coupling strategies. At the same time, with mechanisms such as progress penalty or safety projection, EV feasibility and grid safety can also be integrated into the control framework.

总结来说，我构建了一个多 prosumer 的低压配电网能源管理框架，并将 EV、stationary battery、PV、LSTM forecast、pandapower power flow 和 MADRL training 集成到同一个实验链中。实验表明，MADRL 可以学习到比 rule-based 更灵活的调度策略，尤其是 battery arbitrage 和 EV charging 与电价之间的关系。但 reward-only 的 soft EV constraint 存在 sparse signal 问题，因此 progress penalty 能显著改善 EV feasibility。Hard EV correction 可以更直接地保证 departure requirement，但它和 safety projection 叠加后，会改变 actor action 和 executed action 之间的关系，从而影响学习稳定性和经济性。最终，如果优先考虑经济性，Base Hard 是较好的 MADRL controller；如果优先考虑 EV feasibility 和 grid safety，Projection Hard 是更稳健的选择。未来工作可以从 projection-aware training、更精细的 EV overcharging control，以及更大规模的 prosumer 网络继续扩展。

To summarize, I built a low-voltage distribution-grid energy management framework for multiple prosumers, and integrated EVs, stationary batteries, PV, LSTM forecasts, pandapower power flow, and MADRL training into one experimental pipeline. The results show that MADRL can learn more flexible scheduling strategies than rule-based controllers, especially the relationship between battery arbitrage, EV charging, and electricity price. However, the reward-only soft EV constraint suffers from sparse learning signals, so the progress penalty can significantly improve EV feasibility. Hard EV correction can guarantee the departure requirement more directly, but when it is combined with safety projection, it changes the relationship between actor actions and executed actions, which can affect learning stability and economic performance. In the end, if the priority is economic performance, Base Hard is a good MADRL controller. If the priority is EV feasibility and grid safety, Projection Hard is the more robust choice. Future work can further explore projection-aware training, more refined EV overcharging control, and larger-scale prosumer networks.
